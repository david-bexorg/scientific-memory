# @Claim: Donor age versus calendar time is specification-dependent in this snapshot, not an established trend

### Summary
In the 2026-09-11 `HumanBrainProfile` snapshot, unadjusted donor-level OLS of
age versus continuous preservation time has a negative point estimate whose
95% CI includes zero. That primary model is insufficient evidence of a nonzero
linear trend, and it is also insufficient evidence of no trend. An integer
calendar-year specification is nominally significant and is not a
multiple-testing-adjusted discovery. Yearly means are not monotonic.

### Supporting Evidence
- [[EVD-e35600d8c0ba40d9bf08bc65367e6534]]: Continuous-time OLS CI includes 0; R2 under 1%; death-date sensitivity also includes 0.

### Opposing / Caveat Evidence
- [[EVD-b4e17c90a2d64f1f8c3e5a9b0d7f2e16]]: Integer-year OLS 95% CI excludes 0 (p=0.043), so "no evidence of a trend" is not model-robust.

### Mechanistic Interpretation
This is an observational description of stored donor ages, not a change in the
human population or a causal acquisition-policy effect. Independent unit is
`donor_id` (427 unique rows). Arrival dates are mostly missing or year-1900
placeholders and were correctly unused. Unadjusted OLS cannot separate calendar
time from changing partner/disease mix.
