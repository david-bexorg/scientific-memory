# @Evidence: Integer-year OLS of donor age is nominally significant (p=0.043); yearly means are not monotonic

- **Experimental Context:** Same 412 `HumanBrainProfile` donors as the published continuous-time analysis; age in years; preservation calendar year as the predictor.
- **Perturbation / Exposure:** None. Observational metadata.
- **Quantitative Findings:**
  - Slope -1.637 years of age per calendar year (95% CI -3.224 to -0.049; p=0.0434; R2=0.0099).
  - 2024 mean age 75.09 (n=46) then 2025 mean 75.49 (n=156), so yearly means do not decline each year.
- **Observation:** Whether a linear calendar association is "detected" depends on how time is coded. This does not establish a population age trend.
- **Methodological Caveats:** Unadjusted; 2023 n=6; not a pre-specified primary model; no multiplicity correction.
