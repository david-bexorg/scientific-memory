# @Question: What is the average brain flow in hours 6-12 for the last 30 perfusions?

### Context & Objective
This asks for a descriptive telemetry average of BOS `brain_flow_measured` during
the sixth through twelfth hour after perfusion connection, among the 30 most
recent Cortex perfusions. It is not a quality-control threshold, a target-vs-
measured comparison, or a claim about tissue viability.

### Candidate Claims
- [[CLM-9e7636eb9fbe4b35ab7018f0ebd368fc]]: Cohort mean of per-perfusion means is 161.66 on 24 of 30 recent perfusions.

### Synthesis & Conclusion
As of 2026-09-11, the answer is **161.66** (median of per-perfusion means 166.65)
from 24 contributing perfusions. Six of the last 30 could not be included:
four lacked a canonical BOS timeseries (in-progress or cancelled) and two had
no valid flow samples in the [6, 12) hour window. Bronze BOS does not declare
units for `brain_flow_measured`.
