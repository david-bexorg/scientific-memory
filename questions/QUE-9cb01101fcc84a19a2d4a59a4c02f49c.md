# @Question: Does human donor age change over calendar time?

### Context & Objective
This asks whether stored donor ages in Cortex `HumanBrainProfile` shift across
acquisition years. It is not a question about assayed-sample age, perfusion
quality, or population epidemiology. Primary calendar anchor is tissue
preservation. Arrival dates are mostly null or year-1900 placeholders.
Perfusion overview was previously noted as incomplete for this donor cohort.

### Candidate Claims
- [[CLM-ee9f6c31c96d4c42879799889d9ad679]]: Calendar association is specification-dependent in this snapshot.

### Synthesis & Conclusion
As of 2026-09-11, the answer is inconclusive. Continuous-time OLS does not
exclude a zero slope; integer-year OLS does at p=0.043 without multiplicity
control. Yearly means are not monotonic. Unadjusted donor-level analysis
cannot attribute any slope to biology versus acquisition mix.
